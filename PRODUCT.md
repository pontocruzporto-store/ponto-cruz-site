# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Visitors to Porto, design-conscious travelers, locals looking for thoughtful gifts, and international customers curious about Portuguese craft. They arrive wanting to understand what makes Ponto Cruz different from a generic souvenir shop, discover selected makers, and decide whether the store is worth visiting in person.

## Product Purpose

Ponto Cruz is a family-run concept store in Porto that presents authentic Portuguese craftsmanship, independent brands, and local recommendations with warmth and care. The website should make the shop feel trustworthy, human, and worth seeking out, while helping visitors explore the store, the makers, and nearby Porto experiences in multiple languages.

## Brand Personality

Artesanal, familiar, acolhedora, moderna. The tone should feel personal and curated without becoming precious, polished without feeling cold, and contemporary without losing the sense of handwork and place.

## Anti-references

The site must not feel like a generic tourist souvenir shop, aggressive ecommerce funnel, cold luxury boutique, or hotel template. Avoid fake heritage, overproduced luxury minimalism, stock-photo travel gloss, discount-led retail language, and layouts that make the store feel interchangeable with any Porto guide.

## Design Principles

1. Let the store feel lived-in: show real objects, textures, makers, and Porto context rather than abstract lifestyle promises.
2. Curate before selling: prioritize selection, story, and trust over transactional pressure.
3. Keep the family voice present: copy and structure should feel hosted by real people, not a faceless brand system.
4. Make multilingual travel practical: visitors should quickly understand where to go, what they will find, and why it matters in their language.
5. Balance craft with modern clarity: handmade warmth should sit inside a clean, fast, accessible website.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast and keyboard accessibility across navigation, language switching, forms, and links. Respect reduced-motion preferences, keep mobile touch targets comfortable, avoid relying on color alone for meaning, and preserve readable type for Portuguese, English, Korean, and Japanese content.
